01 · The national vision

In under a decade, the Kingdom built a cyber market by regulation and made it compulsory.

Vision 2030 treats cybersecurity as national infrastructure. The NCA, founded in 2017, writes the controls, sets the standards and funds the talent. Demand is mandated, not discretionary, and it survives the budget cycles that end ordinary IT projects.

Digital sovereignty

Detection, data and response stay in the Kingdom. In-Kingdom SOCs and NDMO data residency are the national objective, not a feature.

Saudi talent

ECC-2:2024 control 1-2-2 requires qualified, full-time Saudi professionals in every cybersecurity role. Every analyst seat is a national high-skill job.

Mandated demand

ECC, OTCC, CCC, NCNICC, the SAMA framework and the PDPL each bind a named population by a named date. Compliance is audited, every year.

Local capability

CyberIC and the National Cybersecurity Academy localise technology and skills. Operators who make the state's objectives their plan grow with it.
